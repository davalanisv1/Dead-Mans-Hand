# 🛠️ July 13th, 2026 - Fifth Build Session: Aft Centering Ring & Avionics Tracker Machining

This session focused on sealing the main booster section and performing precision machining on the recovery hardware components.

## Aft Centering Ring Installation
During the initial motor mount installation, we intentionally left the rear centering ring unbonded. This kept the internal cavity accessible, ensuring that the root tabs of all six split fins could slide completely into place and bond without compromising structural alignment. Now that all fins are permanently anchored, we applied structural epoxy and bonded the final **aft centering ring** flush against the bottom airframe, permanently boxing in the fin can.

## Bulkhead & Payload Sleeve Machining
With the booster section sealed, we moved on to preparing the nose cone payload shoulder components for recovery tracking hardware:
1. **Bulkhead Porting:** Drilled a small pass-through hole into the fiberglass coupler bulkhead. 
2. **Tracker Mount Machining:** Drilled an alignment hole through the inner payload sleeve tracker mount, followed by a manual thread-cutting step using a tap-and-die set. These cut threads allow us to securely bolt down the loaned GPS tracking module for our certification flight.
