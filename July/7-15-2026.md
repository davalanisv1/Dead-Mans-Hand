# 🛠️ July 15th, 2026 - Sixth Build Session: Shear Pin Machining & Tapping

This workshop focused entirely on machining the mechanical retention points across the airframe joints to prevent premature deployment under high flight loads.

## Shear Pin Installation & Tapping
We drilled and manually tapped matching holes through the exterior fiberglass airframe and into the internal couplers for both the avionics bay and the nose cone shoulder. 

These holes will house nylon shear pins during flight. In high-power rocketry, shear pins serve a critical safety function: they mechanically lock the payload and booster sections together, preventing the recovery bays from separating early due to aerodynamic drag or internal vacuum pressures. Upon apogee, the pressure generated by the motor's black powder ejection charge will cleanly snap these nylon pins, allowing the parachute to deploy safely.

* **Nose Cone Joint:** Drilled and tapped to secure the nose cone to the forward payload section.
* **Avionics Bay Joint:** Drilled and tapped to lock the primary coupler assembly to the lower booster airframe.
