# 🛠️ July 27th, 2026 - Seventh Build Session: Injection of Fiber-Reinforced Internal Fillets

This workshop focused on reinforcing the internal structural joints between the root tabs of the six split fins and the inner motor mount tube using an injection fillet technique.

## Internal Fillet Injection Process
Because the fin can is permanently sealed by the centering rings, we utilized the 36 injection ports drilled during our second build session to anchor the fins internally:

1. **Masking & Containment:** Applied blue painter's masking tape over the exterior airframe, exposing only the specific row of holes actively being injected. This isolated the fluid flow and kept the outer fiberglass skin clean.
2. **Matrix Structural Additives:** Mixed high-viscosity structural epoxy and thoroughly integrated milled metallic/fiber strands into the mixture. Adding these chopped fibers dramatically enhances the shear and tensile strength of the cured adhesive.
3. **Syringe Injection:** Injected the reinforced epoxy matrix directly through the ports into the internal fin-to-body cavity, allowing the mixture to settle along the joints and form robust internal fillets.
