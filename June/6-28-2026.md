# 🛠️ June 28th, 2026 - Fourth Build Session: Final Split-Fin Installation

During this brief session, we focused entirely on completing the external aerodynamic surfaces by mounting the final fin cluster.

Following the exact surface preparation protocols established in our prior sessions (80-grit mechanical abrasion followed by thorough acetone and isopropyl alcohol solvent wipes), I prepped and bonded the third and final set of split fins. The fin tabs were coated in structural epoxy, slid into their slots, and locked into perfect alignment using our 3D-printed alignment guide system. 

With this step finished, all six fins are permanently installed and perfectly aligned down the airframe, completing the main structural assembly of the fin can.
