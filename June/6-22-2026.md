# 🛠️ June 22nd, 2026 - First Build Session: Motor Mount Assembly

After waiting a week for a slot in my sponsor's busy schedule—as he was managing build sessions for five students simultaneously—I had my first hands-on build workshop. The session lasted 1.5 hours, and we focused entirely on assembling the core propulsion and recovery anchors.

During this session, we bonded the Kevlar shock cord tether and the G10 centering rings to the 38mm motor mount tube using high-strength epoxy. After the core assembly was finished, my sponsor gave me the option to bevel and shape the edges of the G10 fiberglass fins. He noted that while shaping them poorly could introduce unwanted trailing wake vortices, a clean aerodynamic bevel on the leading edges should reduce drag enough to gain a few hundred additional feet in the apogee.

### Component Functions
* **Kevlar Tether:** Serves as a fire-resistant, high-strength anchor for the rocket's recovery shock cord, engineered to withstand the extreme heat and force of the motor's ejection charge.
* **Centering Rings:** Align and secure the motor mount tube perfectly parallel within the main airframe booster, ensuring the thrust line remains straight and the assembly does not shift during flight.
