# 🧽 June 12th 2026 - Airframe Arrival and Preparation

The Viper 54mm kit arrived at my sponsor's house on June 1st, and I picked it up on June 12th to begin preparation.  

Once home, I soaked and washed the fiberglass components in warm, soapy water in the bathtub, scrubbing them gently with a towel before drying and bagging them. This step is critical for high-power fiberglass kits: it removes the residual manufacturing mold release compound. If left on the surface, the epoxy would fail to adhere properly, risking structural failure under flight loads.
