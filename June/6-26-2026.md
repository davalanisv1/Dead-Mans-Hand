# 🛠️ June 26th, 2026 - Third Build Session: Coupler Assemblies & Second Fin Set Installation

During our third hands-on workshop, focus shifted toward assembling the modular sub-assemblies of the airframe, prepping the payload separation points, and continuing the external fin installation.

## Airframe & Recovery Coupler Assemblies
* **Avionics Bay Coupler:** Bonded the cosmetic switch band onto the center of the fiberglass electronics bay coupler using structural epoxy. This allows the lower booster tube and upper payload tube to join seamlessly along the exterior airframe.
* **Nose Cone Integration:** Permanently epoxied the nose cone shoulder into the base of the 5:1 ogive nose cone, creating a rigid singular unit that slides smoothly into the forward payload tube.
* **Payload Retention:** Drilled alignment hardware holes through the upper payload body tube and into the inner coupler shoulder to seat PEM nuts. These threaded inserts provide a secure anchor point to bolt the payload bay shut during flight.

## Second Fin Set Installation
Following the same chemical preparation protocol used in the previous session (mechanical abrasion, followed by acetone and isopropyl alcohol solvent wipes), I prepped and installed the second set of split fins. The fins were epoxied into their slots and locked straight using our 3D-printed alignment guide system.
