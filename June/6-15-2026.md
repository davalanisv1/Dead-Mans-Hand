# 🧽 June 15th, 2026 - Airframe Arrival & Preparation

The Viper 54mm kit arrived at my sponsor's house on June 1st, and I picked it up on June 15th to begin preparation. 

Once home, I soaked and washed the fiberglass components in warm, soapy water in the bathtub, scrubbing them gently with a towel before drying and bagging them. This step is critical for high-power fiberglass kits: it removes the residual manufacturing mold release compound. If left on the surface, the epoxy would fail to adhere properly, risking structural failure under flight loads.

Concurrently, I had begun reading the *Handbook of Model Rocketry* by G. Harry Stine and *Modern High-Power Rocketry* by Mark Canepa to deepen my technical understanding of aerospace dynamics and safety standards as I entered the hobby.
