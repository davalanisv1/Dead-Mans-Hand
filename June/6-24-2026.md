# 🔩 June 24th, 2026 - Second Build Session: Airframe Drilling, Rail Hardware, & Motor Mount Integration

Two days after completing the core motor mount assembly, I returned to the workshop. This session focused on precision drilling of the airframe, installing internal mounting hardware, and bonding the motor mount assembly into the booster.

We drilled a total of 37 holes into the lower booster section to accommodate the rocket's unique 6-fin split-fin design (three forward fins and three aft fins). Thirty-six of these holes—three on each side of all six fin slots—serve as injection ports for future internal epoxy fillets. The final booster hole was drilled near the bottom to house the lower 1010-size launch rail button. A separate rail button hole was drilled into the upper payload body tube to provide a wide, stable stance on the launch rail.

After drilling, I applied high-strength epoxy to the flanges of the PEM nuts (threaded inserts) to secure them inside the rail button mounting holes from the interior of the tubes. Finally, I applied structural epoxy to the interior of the airframe and slid the motor mount assembly into place, taking care to align the components perfectly while keeping the excess epoxy from smearing the exterior airframe.
