# 🛠️ August 22nd, 2026 - Tenth Build Session: External Fillets & OpenRocket Simulations

This session focused on reinforcing the external airframe junctions where the fins meet the lower body tube. Using epoxy resin and a rounded fondant tool, I applied smooth external fillets. While these fillets have a minimal impact on overall aerodynamics—primarily just reducing laminar flow disruption—they significantly improve structural integrity at the root joints and add a highly aesthetic, professional finish recommended by my mentor.

I ran a new series of flight simulations in OpenRocket to evaluate rocket stability and performance under varying environmental conditions:

- Baseline Winds: 9.4 mph to 10.4 mph (the average statewide wind speed for Michigan, verified via AI data).
- High-Wind Stress Test: 15.0 mph to simulate higher-than-normal launch day gusts.

Based on design feedback from my mentor, I updated the rocket's color scheme. To save construction time, I originally planned to paint only the switchband and nose cone red, leaving the rest of the body black. However, my mentor highlighted that painting the fins red would drastically improve aesthetics. I accepted this advice and updated the design.

Target Launch Date: September 26th, 2026.
