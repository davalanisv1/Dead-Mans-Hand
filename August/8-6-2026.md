# 🛠️ August 6th, 2026 - Eighth Build Session:  Internal Fillets & NAR Certification Exam

This workshop marked a double milestone: completing the 2/3 of internal structural reinforcement of the propulsion section and clearing the formal academic requirements for high-power certification.

## Phase 2: Internal Fillet Injection Completed
Following the exact protocol from our previous workshop, we mixed a second batch of high-viscosity structural epoxy blended with milled structural fiber strands. We masked, isolated, and injected twelve of internal ports. With this session concluded, 66% of the internal joints within the sealed booster cavity are fully reinforced, creating an incredibly strong fin can capable of handling high-velocity aerodynamic stress.

## 🎓 NAR Junior Level 1 Exam Passed
Outside of the physical build, I successfully completed and passed the **National Association of Rocketry (NAR) Junior High-Power Rocketry Level 1 Exam**. Passing this test demonstrates a firm understanding of high-power rocketry safety protocols, motor classifications, and FAA airspace regulations, officially clearing me for flight operations on launch day.
