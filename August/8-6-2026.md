# 🛠️ August 6th, 2026 - Eighth Build Session: Final Internal Fillets & NAR Certification Exam

This workshop marked a double milestone: completing the internal structural reinforcement of the propulsion section and clearing the formal academic requirements for high-power certification.

## Phase 2: Internal Fillet Injection Completed
Following the exact protocol from our previous workshop, we mixed a second batch of high-viscosity structural epoxy blended with milled structural fiber strands. We masked, isolated, and injected the remaining 18 internal ports to complete the fillets for the opposite side of all six split fins. With this session concluded, all internal joints within the sealed booster cavity are fully reinforced, creating an incredibly strong fin can capable of handling high-velocity aerodynamic stress.

## 🎓 NAR Junior Level 1 Exam Passed
Outside of the physical build, I successfully completed and passed the **National Association of Rocketry (NAR) Junior High-Power Rocketry Level 1 Exam**. Passing this test demonstrates a firm understanding of high-power rocketry safety protocols, motor classifications, and FAA airspace regulations, officially clearing me for flight operations on launch day.
